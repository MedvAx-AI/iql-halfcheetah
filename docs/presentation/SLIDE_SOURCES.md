# Slide sources

The short PowerPoint notes cite these numbered references. These references are not spoken narration.

1. **IQL method:** Kostrikov, Nair and Levine, *Offline Reinforcement Learning with Implicit Q-Learning*. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.06169), [official implementation](https://github.com/ikostrikov/implicit_q_learning). Used on slides 1-3 and 7.
2. **HalfCheetah-v5:** Gymnasium's [environment documentation](https://gymnasium.farama.org/environments/mujoco/half_cheetah/). Observation/action spaces, default reward and episode ending. Used on slides 4, 5 and 7.
3. **Project settings and protocol:** [Configuration](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/configs/halfcheetah.toml), [IQL implementation](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/src/iql_project/iql.py), [continuation protocol and measured evidence](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/docs/POLICY_CONTINUATION.md). Used on slides 3, 5-7.
4. **Environment images:** fresh local 1920 x 1080 MuJoCo 3.2.3 simulator renders using training seed 1 at 500k, evaluation seed 10000, at steps 0 and 400 (20 simulated seconds). Only the camera and rendering dimensions differ from the evaluation environment. These are local simulator images, not captured Colab video frames. [Initial state](assets/halfcheetah-0.png), [running state](assets/halfcheetah-400.png), [checkpoint hash and render provenance](assets/provenance.json). Used on slides 1 and 4. The chart and final-test measurements remain the recorded Colab evidence in reference 6.
5. **Offline dataset:** Minari [HalfCheetah medium dataset](https://minari.farama.org/datasets/mujoco/halfcheetah/medium-v0/). [Measured local dataset report](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/docs/evidence/simulator-3.2.3/dataset_report.json). Used on slide 6.
6. **Results chart:** [Recorded Colab validation means](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/docs/evidence/continuation/validation/summary.json), [independent final test](https://github.com/MedvAx-AI/iql-halfcheetah/blob/c6113eed1183ccc40da9fb3e9074ec9471eca8a2/docs/evidence/continuation/independent-final-test.json). Slide 8 uses all three training seeds, five measured checkpoints, ten evaluation episodes per point, and two-decimal chart data. Lines connect measurements and do not estimate unmeasured checkpoints. The separate final-test means are 15,260.36 at 500k and 2,026.01 at 100k for training seed 1, each measured over ten new episodes. A long stationary tail is at least five seconds at the end with absolute forward velocity at most 0.1 m/s. The tail count is descriptive rather than a benchmark success score. Other training seeds still fail, and these samples do not guarantee success everywhere.

Slide 5's reward example is illustrative: `0.1 * 6 * 0.5^2 = 0.15` control cost and `5 - 0.15 = 4.85` reward. It is not a measured episode.
